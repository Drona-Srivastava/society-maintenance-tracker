1. Smart Complaint Classification

When a resident submits:

"Water leaking from the ceiling in my bedroom."

The backend could automatically classify:

Category: Plumbing
Priority: High
Estimated SLA: 24 hours

Whereas:

"The garden lights near Block B aren't working."

becomes:

Category: Electrical
Priority: Medium
Estimated SLA: 3 days

You could initially implement this with a lightweight ML model/rules and later replace it with an actual classifier.

This gives you a legitimate ML component to discuss rather than adding AI just for the sake of saying "AI".

2. Complaint SLA + Escalation

You already have COMPLAINT_OVERDUE_DAYS=3.

Take that further.

Each complaint gets:

Created
   ↓
Assigned
   ↓
In Progress
   ↓
Resolved
   ↓
Resident confirms
   ↓
Closed

If:

current_time > SLA

automatically mark:

OVERDUE

Then surface it to the admin:

5 complaints require attention

This is much stronger than simply showing a complaints table.
